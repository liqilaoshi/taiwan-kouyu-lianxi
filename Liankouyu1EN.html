Interactive Grammar Speaking: 「怎麼 (zěnme)」

Asking & Describing How Actions Are Done

Pattern Subject + 可以 + 怎麼 + Verb (去 + Place)?

Place 「怎麼 (zěnme)」 before a verb to ask "how" to do something or which transportation to take. To answer, simply replace 「怎麼」 with a mode of transport (e.g., 坐高鐵, 搭捷運, 騎摩托車)!

Question Example: 安同可以怎麼去學校？ How can Antong go to school?
Answer Example: 安同可以搭捷運（或坐公車）去學校。 Antong can take the MRT (or take the bus) to school.
